# Template: One-pager

- **File**: `templates/one-pager.html`
- **Formats**: PDF (primary), DOCX
- **Use for**: a one-page overview of The AI Rundown, George or one offer.
- **Length**: exactly one page in the PDF.

## Structure

1. Compact header.
2. Eyebrow, H1 (`THE AI RUNDOWN`), lead line, one short paragraph with the
   format and cadence.
3. `The show`: table `Segment`, `What it covers` with the four segments
   from the guidelines, in order.
4. `The numbers`: table `Metric`, `Value`, `Period`.
5. Ask box: one clear ask pointing to Orage AI Agency.

## Content

- Audience numbers only from the request; otherwise keep the placeholders.
- Cut words before shrinking type. Check the PDF page count is 1.
