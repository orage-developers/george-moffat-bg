# Template: Slides

- **File**: `templates/slides.html`
- **Formats**: PPTX, PDF
- **Use for**: any deck without a closer template.
- **Also follow**: [format-pptx.md](format-pptx.md).

## Structure

1. Cover (`slide cover`): eyebrow, H1 in Anton (second line in
   `<span class="thin">` grey faint), rule, lead line.
2. Content slides: eyebrow `NN — <topic>`, H2, rule, then bullets, a stat
   callout (`.big` number with a card), or columns. Footer with the name and
   the slide number.
3. Closing slide (`slide cover`): H2 with one clear ask and an arrow, lead
   line pointing to Orage.
