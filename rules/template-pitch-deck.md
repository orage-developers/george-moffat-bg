# Template: Pitch deck

- **File**: `templates/pitch-deck.html`
- **Formats**: PPTX, PDF
- **Use for**: The AI Rundown decks for partners, sponsors and guests.
- **Also follow**: [format-pptx.md](format-pptx.md).

## Structure

1. Cover: `THE AI RUNDOWN`, cadence line.
2. The host: George's positioning in three bullets.
3. The show: the four segments as cards (`grid2`). The lead segment has the
   white bar (`card`); the others are standard (`card std`).
4. The audience: three stat cards (`grid3`) with Anton numbers.
5. The offer: one idea with a price card.
6. Closing: one clear ask with an arrow and the booking link.

## Content

- Show facts (format, cadence, segments) only from the guidelines.
- Audience numbers, offers and prices only from the request; otherwise keep
  the placeholders.
