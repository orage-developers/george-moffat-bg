# Template: Proposal

- **File**: `templates/proposal.html`
- **Formats**: DOCX, PDF
- **Use for**: proposals to partners, sponsors or clients.

## Structure

1. Compact header.
2. Eyebrow: `Proposal · <partner>`. H1: the outcome for the partner. Lead
   line with the result and the date. Meta line: prepared for, date,
   validity.
3. `Where you are`: the partner's constraint in their words and numbers.
4. `What we do`: one segment card per deliverable (eyebrow, H3, one line).
   The white left bar marks each card.
5. `Investment`: price box (full white border) with package, price and
   terms.
6. `Timeline`: table `Week`, `Milestone`.
7. `Next step`: one CTA line with `<span class="arrow">→</span>`.

## Content

- Uses the compact type scale: H1 24pt, H2 15pt, H3 12.5pt, body 11pt,
  price 20pt. Keep sizes consistent; do not enlarge single elements.
- Prices, deliverables and terms come only from the request. Leave the
  placeholders when they are not given.
