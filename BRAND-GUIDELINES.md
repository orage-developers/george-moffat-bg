# George W. Moffat — Personal Brand Book

**Version 1.0 · 2026.** The personal brand behind The AI Rundown. One signal,
no noise: pure black, pure white, nothing wasted.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Host | George W. Moffat |
| Show | The AI Rundown |
| Cadence | Tuesdays · YouTube Live |
| Anchor | `#FFFFFF` |

## 1. Identity

These are the non-visual fundamentals every personal artifact rests on. This
brand carries the show and pushes Orage. It is not a separate channel
persona.

- **Person:** George W. Moffat
- **The show:** The AI Rundown
- **Format:** Weekly talk-to-camera YouTube Live. Current AI news, viral
  posts, and the jobs fight, delivered straight to the lens.
- **Positioning:** The operator who tells you what actually happened in AI
  this week and why it changes how you work.
- **Voice:** Direct · authoritative · results-first · high-status calm.
  Reference point: Hormozi / acquisition.com.
- **Relationship to Orage:** Personal brand first. Every show and asset
  points back to Orage AI Agency.

## 2. Visual system: one system, monochrome

The company brand runs two modes and a gold anchor. The personal brand runs
**one**: strict black and white. There is no second mode and no accent color
to choose.

**The monochrome rule: black, white, greyscale.** Pure black backgrounds.
White is the single accent. Greys carry every level of hierarchy. Emphasis
comes from a soft white glow and from scale, never from color.

It applies to:

- Stream overlays, lower thirds, and bugs
- The AI Rundown broadcast brief
- Thumbnails, titles, and end cards
- Slides and short-form clips
- Any document carrying George's name

## 3. Voice

Sound like a senior operator handing over the takeaway, not a creator
begging for the click. Lead with the result. Cut every line by 30% before it
goes to air.

| Off-brand | On-brand |
|---|---|
| "Hey guys, welcome back to another video, hope you're all doing great!" | "Three things happened in AI this week that change how you work. Let's go." |
| "This is so crazy and revolutionary, it's a total game-changer!" | "Biggest startup acquisition in history. Here's why it lands on your desk." |
| "I think maybe AI could possibly affect some jobs eventually." | "One company cut 21,000 roles this week and named AI. Here's who's next." |
| "Don't forget to smash that like and subscribe button!" | "If this saved you an hour, subscribe. Next week's saves you more." |

## 4. Typography: two fonts, no exceptions

Anton for the heaviest display moments. Bebas Neue for everything else:
subheads, labels, body, buttons, captions. Never introduce a third typeface.
This is the most strictly enforced rule in the system.

| Font | Role | Case | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anton** | Display / hero | Always uppercase | 1–2 px |
| **Bebas Neue** | Everything else | Always uppercase | 2–6 px |

### Specimens

| Use | Font and treatment |
|---|---|
| Hero | Anton · 72 px · tracking 2 px · soft white glow |
| Lower third / stat | Anton · 44 px · tracking 1 px |
| Subhead | Bebas Neue · 30 px · tracking 2 px |
| Eyebrow / label | Bebas Neue · 13 px · tracking 5 px |
| Body | Bebas Neue · 17 px · tracking .03em |

Body and talking points use Bebas Neue, uppercase and tracked, with
greyscale carrying the hierarchy. Keep lines short: the system reads as a
broadcast, not a paragraph.

## 6. Components

A fixed kit. Combine and repeat; never invent new ones. Every component is
pure black and white, glow for emphasis, no emoji.

- **On-air bug:** white outline, pulsing white dot, top-left of the frame,
  always present live.
- **Lower third:** Anton name, Bebas role, white left rule with a soft glow,
  locked layout. Example: "George W. Moffat" / "The AI Rundown · Founder,
  Orage".
- **Buttons:** primary is a white fill with black text; ghost is a white
  outline. One primary per view.
- **Stat callout:** Anton numeral in white with glow, Bebas label beneath.
  Example: "$60B" / "Biggest startup acquisition ever".
- **Segment card:** white left bar for the lead story, grey for standard
  ones; tag, heat, headline, summary.

## 7. Broadcast structure: show anatomy

Every Rundown follows this top-to-bottom structure. Segments can be trimmed
or extended, but the order and the four-segment spine never change.

1. **Cold open:** lead story in the first ten seconds. No throat-clearing
   intro. Hook, then name the show.
2. **Bug + timer:** the on-air bug and the running stopwatch stay on screen
   the whole show for pacing.
3. **01 · Headlines:** the biggest verified AI news of the week. Open hot.
4. **02 · Viral & spicy:** contested posts and hot takes. Read aloud, react,
   take a side.
5. **03 · Reddit rabbit holes:** threads worth reacting to live. Title, top
   comment, verdict.
6. **04 · The big fight:** jobs and the economy. Two clashing sides,
   debate-style close.
7. **Lower third:** name and Orage credit present throughout. The brand
   never leaves the frame.
8. **Close + CTA:** one clear ask. Point back to Orage. No begging, no
   filler.

## 8. Non-negotiables

Twelve absolute rules. Breaking any one breaks the brand.

1. No color, ever. **Black, white, greyscale** only.
2. No gold. The company anchor **`#B68039`** never appears in the personal
   brand.
3. Two fonts only: **Anton + Bebas Neue**. No third typeface.
4. No emoji anywhere: headings, cards, overlays, captions, thumbnails.
5. Bebas is always uppercase, always tracked. Never lowercase.
6. White is the only accent. Emphasis comes from **glow and scale**, not
   hue.
7. Pure black backgrounds on every on-screen and document surface.
8. One primary action per view. One white button; the rest are ghost.
9. No hype words. "Revolutionary," "game-changing," "insane" are banned.
10. One primary graphic on screen at a time. Never cover the face.
11. Personal brand first: every asset points back to **Orage**.
12. Live authenticity. Real screenshots captured on stream, never faked.
